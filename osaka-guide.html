Good to know

Small things that save the trip
💡

Sunday closures: Kiwami-dori Ebisu and Sushi Kiwami are closed Sun; Tsurugyu and Bear Paw Cafe are closed Mon; teamLab is closed Oct 26.

💴

Cash: tachinomi standing bars and small izakayas are often cash-only. Keep yen on hand for bar crawls.

🚄

KIX on Monday: Nankai Rapi:t from Namba to Kansai Airport is ~35 min. Leave Shinsaibashi by ~2:30pm to be at the airport by 4pm.
